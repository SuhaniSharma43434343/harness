# AI slop in website design

## The numbers and labels habit (01, 02, 03)

This is the one you asked about. It is easy to add and it makes a page feel designed, so AI does it everywhere.

- **Numbered steps** such as 1, 2, 3 or 01, 02, 03 for a process. Fine when order really matters, a tell when it is just decoration. One catalog lists "meaningless numbered lists" for exactly this reason.
- **Big faded numbers behind section titles**, like "01 — About us". My read: very common in AI output, though I found no source that names it on its own.
- **A small tag above the headline**, in capitals ("FEATURES", "HOW IT WORKS"). Audits call this the eyebrow badge.
- **A row of big stats**, "10,000+ customers, 99% satisfaction, 24/7 support". Round numbers nobody can check.
- **Tiny decorative charts** that look like data but say nothing.

What to do instead: number things only when order matters, drop the tag above headings, and show a real fact with its source or show nothing.

## Layout

- **The centred hero.** Headline, one line under it, two buttons, everything in the middle. Nothing says what this business does.
- **Three equal cards in a row**, each with an icon on top. The single most common tell.
- **Cards inside cards**, and a card around every piece of content.
- **A coloured stripe on one edge** of a card.
- **Same spacing everywhere**, so the page has no rhythm.
- **Two buttons of equal weight**, so the visitor does not know the main action.
- **The same section order every time:** hero, features, stats, testimonials, pricing, FAQ, banner, footer.

Instead: put the one thing this visitor wants to do right at the top, let sections have different sizes and shapes, and break the grid on purpose somewhere.

## Colour and surface

- **Purple-to-blue gradients.** Audits nicknamed it "VibeCode purple".
- **Dark mode with glowing accents**, cyan or neon on near-black.
- **Gradient text** on headlines.
- **Glassmorphism:** blurred, see-through panels on everything.
- **Glow shadows** and soft shadows on every box.
- **Pure black and pure white**, with no tint.
- **Grey text on coloured backgrounds**, which looks washed out.
- **One corner radius for every element.**

Instead: take the palette from the business. A garage, a cafe and an automation studio should not share colours.

## Type

- **Inter for everything**, one weight, one size jump.
- **The usual AI font pairings:** Space Grotesk, Instrument Serif, Geist.
- **One italic serif word** dropped into a sans headline.
- **Monospace on normal text** to look "techy".
- **Capitals and Title Case everywhere.**

Instead: choose a typeface that fits the brand, and give the page real contrast between sizes and weights.

## Words

AI copy has a rhythm you learn to hear.

- **Filler verbs:** empower, leverage, unlock, elevate, seamless, effortless.
- **Mission lines** that could belong to any company: "revolutionising the way you work".
- **Triplets:** "Fast. Simple. Powerful."
- **A welcome paragraph** before anything useful.
- **An intro that repeats its own heading.**
- **Made-up testimonials:** a Jane Doe, a stock face and praise that names nothing.
- **Generic buttons:** "Learn more", "Get started", "Submit".
- **Leftover placeholder text:** lorem ipsum, "coming soon", TODO.

Instead: use the owner's own facts, real numbers with sources, and buttons that say what happens ("Call us", "Book a table").

## Pictures and icons

- **Stock photos** of smiling people at laptops, unrelated to the product.
- **Abstract blobs or 3D shapes** standing in for a real image.
- **Emoji used as icons**, including in menus.
- **The same icon in a circle** above every feature.
- **A fake dashboard screenshot.**
- **Mixed icon styles** in one site.

## Motion

- **Everything fades up** as you scroll.
- **Parallax on every section.**
- **Bouncy easing.**
- **Things that animate forever:** pulses, bobbing arrows.
- **The same hover-zoom on every image.**
- **A popup for something that could be inline.**
- **Video that plays with sound without being asked.**

Instead: one or two moments of motion that mean something, and nothing else.

## Mobile and basics

- Desktop layout squeezed into one column, with tiny tap targets.
- Features hidden on small screens.
- No hover, focus or disabled states.
- Error messages that blame the visitor.

## The giveaway combinations

One catalog notes that a single habit is not the problem. The combination is: purple gradient plus Inter plus centred layout plus three cards; or fade-up plus parallax plus decorative motion; or round-number stats plus a generic mission line.
