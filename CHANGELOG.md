# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

- 3D and video (`src/media/`, built from the 3D and video proposal): after research and before the approach is chosen, `forge go` judges honestly whether a 3D object suits the business (from the playbook's own rating, the tone, the main action and the device floor) and whether video helps, and asks. 3D is never added without a yes. If the fit is poor the operator can continue without 3D (our analysis) or include it anyway; then own model (.glb, 3 MB or less) or the harness searches Poly Haven (CC0) and shows three to choose from; nothing is downloaded before the pick. A supplied video is analysed (ffprobe) and placed by rule: a short wide clip is a hero loop behind a scrim, a medium one a tap-to-play story block, an upright one a framed block, a long one is cut down; the operator confirms or changes the place. Web versions (MP4, WebM, poster) are made with ffmpeg, silent, and every file gets a licence register row. Free clips come from Pexels with a key. The choice is saved in `design/media-decision.json` and `brief.media`; the section plan, asset plan, copy and build contracts use it, the plan checks refuse a 3D object in the hero or media left unplaced, and the build adds the Scene3D section as an accent without changing the approach. The brief schema gains an optional `media` section (a frozen M1 interface: needs a pull request approved by both owners). Flags: `--3d yes|no|<file.glb>`, `--pick <id>`, `--video <file>`, `--video-role`, `--no-media`.
- Craft guide (`registry/craft/awwwards.md`): how well the design must be done, judged the way Awwwards judges (Design 40%, Usability 30%, Creativity 20%, Content 10%), with the list of tells that make a site look machine-made. It is copied into every project and read by the direction, design-system, section-plan, motion-plan, copy, specification-review and visual-review tasks, whose contracts now carry its rules (poster-scale type, a signature moment per page, grid-breaking compositions, motion with a job, plain-English specific copy). The review scores the four criteria and treats under 7 as blocking. New automatic checks refuse a plan before anything is built: a home page with fewer than 7 sections (5 on a small site), an inner page with fewer than 4, fewer than a third of sections breaking the grid, a centred hero that breaks nothing, and a motion plan with fewer than 5 motions, fewer than 3 kinds or nothing scroll-linked.

- Visitor questions (`registry/questions/story.json`): where visitors come from, and which device they use, in `brief.business`. The direction stage must rest its concept on one of the owner's `truths` (from the existing `q-truths` question), the copy must use them and register each as a claim, and the first screen is designed for how visitors arrive. `forge go` asks the `q-truths` question at a terminal (Enter skips it); an unknown is never presented as a fact. Domain access, legal lines and the deadline use the existing `access`, `contentRules` and `constraints.deadline` fields.
- One-prompt mode: `forge go <project> "<what the website is for>"` takes one sentence, reads the business name, what it does, the kind of site and the size from the words (`src/intake/express.mjs`), uses the playbook's recommendation for every other question as a recorded assumption, and runs intake and the design workflow in one go. It stops only for what the operator owns: B1, the design direction (B2), B3 and a licence decision, each settled by a yes typed at the terminal or by `--approve B1,B3` and `--direction <id>`. It never records an approval itself. Then it runs visual QA. Spend is capped at $25 unless `--max-usd` says otherwise. `--deep` runs the full interview instead. `forge design choose` now shares its code with it (`src/design/choose.mjs`).
- Industry playbooks (`registry/industries/`): intake detects the industry from the owner's own words and asks that industry's questions in place of the generic ones, recommends a full page set for the tier (5, 10 to 11 and 13 to 15 pages), and records the one action the business exists for (`brief.business`). The home hero must carry it: the section-plan and copy checks fail otherwise, and the design stages are told. Five playbooks: real estate, restaurant or cafe, clinic or healthcare, professional services and a general business fallback. See `docs/industry-playbooks.md`. The brief schema gains an optional `business` section (a frozen M1 interface: needs a pull request approved by both owners).

- Intake: typed answers match an option by number, value, label (any case) or the start of a label, and an answer that fits no option or several is asked again at once. Before, typing "up to 12 pages" or "AAA" failed the whole round with SCHEMA_INVALID after the last question.
- The planner: `forge plan` (and every `forge run`) writes the task contracts for the phases whose work is the agents': research, page content (seeded from the site's draft pages) and the asset register, each with a `forge check` verify command. This is what gave the first real-model run its work. It ran on 2026-10-29 on the sample business site: research, content and the asset register on a small model, about $0.04 for the first two.
- Phase exits and the last commands: `forge advance` moves a project on only when the phase's tasks, evidence and approval are all there (refusals name what is missing), and `forge run --advance` keeps going until an approval or a blocker. `forge preview` (local, after a secret scan), `forge deploy --preview|--production` (vercel, with the form function and a smoke test), `forge report` (the final report from the ledger), `forge test --mock` and `forge demo` (the sample project to a local preview, no model or paid API). No command is listed as planned any more.

## Design workflow (2026-11-02)

- Each of the 8 approaches is a complete methodology in `registry/methodologies/`, and a site follows one.
- A full design specification is required before the build: direction, design system, section, motion and asset plans, and real copy.
- New Design Manager agent, `forge design status|run|qa`, and the `design-fidelity` and `visual-review` gates.
