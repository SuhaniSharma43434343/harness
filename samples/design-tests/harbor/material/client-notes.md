# Harbor Home Renovations: client notes (sample client for testing)

This is an invented test client. Everything below counts as the client's own statements.

- Family firm in Portland, Maine, founded in 2009 by Ruth Almeida, a former ship's carpenter. Her son Tomas runs the site teams now.
- 11 people, all employed directly: three carpenters, two tilers, a plasterer, an electrician, a plumber, two site leads and Ruth. No subcontractors except for roofing.
- Work: kitchens, bathrooms, whole-floor renovations, decks and porches. About 40 projects a year, within 30 miles of Portland.
- The fixed quote: every job gets an itemised written quote after a free home visit. The price changes only if the client changes the plan, in writing. Ruth says: "If we priced it wrong, that's our problem, not yours."
- One site lead per job, who is there every working day and sends a short text update each evening.
- Dust and mess: sealed doorways, floor protection, swept clean at 4:30pm every day. Clients often stay at home during the work.
- Old houses are their speciality: a lot of Portland's housing is over 100 years old, with crooked walls and old wiring. Ruth's ship-carpentry background means they are comfortable where nothing is square.
- Typical times: a bathroom takes 2 to 3 weeks, a kitchen 4 to 6 weeks, a deck about 8 working days.
- Guarantee: 5-year workmanship guarantee in writing on all work.
- Licensed and insured in Maine.
- Contact: Ruth answers the phone herself: (207) 555-0142. Email: hello@harborhome.example. Workshop at 18 Commercial Street, Portland, Maine. Monday to Friday, 7:30 to 4:30.
- Three past jobs that may be described:
  1. Munjoy Hill kitchen: an 1890s row house, a closed-off galley kitchen opened to the dining room, a beam hidden in the ceiling, 5 weeks.
  2. Deering bathroom: a 1920s upstairs bathroom with a leaking floor, rebuilt with a freestanding tub and full waterproofing, 16 working days.
  3. Back Cove deck: a sloping garden levelled with a two-tier cedar deck, 8 working days.
- Client words they have allowed us to use: "They told us what it would cost, and that is what it cost." (the Hallorans, Munjoy Hill). "I worked from home upstairs the whole time. I forgot they were there, until the kitchen appeared." (Dana W., Deering)
- They dislike: glossy magazine-style renovation marketing, "dream home" language, stock smiling families.
